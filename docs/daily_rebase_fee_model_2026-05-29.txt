Daily Rebase Fee Model - 2026-05-29

Goal
- Charge only on current UTC day positive realized PnL.
- Start counting at exact client fecha_inicio timestamp.
- Rebase next-day operational capital from today's close.

Business Rules
1) Counting starts at fecha_inicio exactly.
2) First day can be partial (from onboarding timestamp).
3) Daily service fee only on current UTC day positive PnL.
4) Next day capital base = operational capital today + today PnL.
5) No re-charging on gains from prior days.

Formulas
- service_due_today = 0.20 * max(today_pnl_utc, 0)
- client_net_today = today_pnl_utc - service_due_today
- operational_capital_today = capital_inicial + pnl_before_today_utc
- projected_next_day_capital = operational_capital_today + today_pnl_utc
